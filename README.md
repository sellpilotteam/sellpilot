#  SellPilotAfrica

**AI-powered commerce and payment infrastructure for African merchants.**

SellPilotAfrica helps informal businesses and small-to-medium enterprises manage sales, customer engagement, and payments across social commerce platforms such as WhatsApp, Instagram, and Telegram.

Powered by the Stellar network and stablecoins like USDC, SellPilotAfrica is designed to make digital commerce faster, more reliable, and accessible across Africa.

## The Problem

Many African merchants face challenges that affect daily sales and business growth:

* Fake payment alerts and fraudulent transaction confirmations
* Delayed bank transfers and payment settlement
* Manual order and inventory tracking
* Poor customer follow-up and retention
* Limited access to international and multi-currency payment infrastructure
* Difficulty managing sales across multiple social media channels

These challenges lead to lost revenue, inefficient operations, and limited opportunities to serve customers across borders.

##  What Is SellPilotAfrica?

SellPilotAfrica is a merchant-focused commerce platform that combines payments, automation, and AI-powered customer engagement in one dashboard.

Merchants can manage orders, receive payments, automate customer interactions, and monitor business activity without relying entirely on manual processes.

##  Key Features

###  Instant Payments

Enable merchants to receive and verify payments quickly using Stellar-powered stablecoin transactions.

###  Subscription Billing

Support recurring payments for merchants offering memberships, digital services, subscriptions, or installment-based products.

###  AI-Powered Customer Engagement

Automate customer follow-ups, product inquiries, reminders, and sales conversations.

### Multi-Currency Settlements

Support cross-border commerce using digital currencies and stablecoins such as USDC.

###  Merchant Dashboard

Provide a centralized dashboard for managing orders, payments, customers, subscriptions, and transaction history.

###  Sales and Payment Analytics

Help merchants track revenue, successful transactions, pending payments, and customer activity.

## 🔗Why Stellar?

Stellar provides the infrastructure for fast, low-cost, and accessible digital payments.

SellPilotAfrica leverages Stellar to explore:

* Fast transaction settlement
* Low-cost payment processing
* Stablecoin-based commerce
* Transparent transaction verification
* Cross-border payment capabilities
* Accessible financial infrastructure for African businesses

* Transaction verification and payment status tracking

## Long-Term Vision

SellPilotAfrica aims to become a trusted commerce and payment infrastructure layer for African merchants, enabling them to sell through social channels, automate operations, and receive payments locally and internationally.

The long-term vision is to connect African businesses to a more open, efficient, and borderless digital economy.
